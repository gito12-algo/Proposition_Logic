# Proposition Logic:
The simplest type of logic. A world of True,False.

# What is Propositions?
A proposition is a declarative statement which is either true or false, but not both simultaneously.(Propositions are somethings called 'statements') Examples of propositions are:

- This rose is white.
- Triangles have four vertices.
- 3 + 2 =4
- 6 < 24
- Tomorrow is my birthday.
 
# What is "Not a Proposition" 
Exclamations, questions and demands are not propositions since they cannot be declared true or false. Thus the following are not propositions.

- Keep off the grass
- Long live the Queen!
- Did you go to Janes's party?
- Don't say that.
